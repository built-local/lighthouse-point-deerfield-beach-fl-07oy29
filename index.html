Common Questions About Chiropractic Care

Is chiropractic care safe?

When performed by a licensed professional, chiropractic adjustments are generally safe. Some patients experience mild muscle soreness or stiffness after treatment—sensations that typically fade within a day. Serious complications are rare, but certain individuals with severe osteoporosis, spinal infections, or specific vascular conditions should avoid forceful neck manipulation. A thorough consultation helps identify whether adjustments are appropriate for your health profile.

How often should I visit?

Frequency depends on your injury severity and recovery phase. Acute pain often requires 2 to 3 visits weekly initially, then tapers to 1 to 2 visits per week during correction and healing. Once improvement stabilizes, maintenance visits may drop to once or twice monthly to prevent old problems from returning. Your chiropractor will create a schedule tailored to your condition and how your body responds to treatment.

What are the risks?

Most people tolerate adjustments without issue. Temporary soreness is normal. Rare severe complications—such as herniated discs or nerve compression—do exist but occur infrequently under professional care. People with weakened bones, spinal tumors, or high stroke risk should discuss concerns with their doctor before beginning treatment. This is why a detailed health history during your first visit matters.
